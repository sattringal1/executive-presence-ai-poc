The Vision

Executive Presence AI was born from a singular vision: authentic leadership is not accidental; it is intentional. We aim to demystify the elusive concept of "executive presence" by using intelligent automation to provide the feedback looping managers have always lacked.

The Solution

This product bridges the gap between everyday communication and strategic influence. By connecting to your Microsoft 365 graph (Emails, Teams, Meetings), our Agentic AI agent analyzes context rather than just content. It acts as a real-time leadership coach, guiding you through communication reframing, meeting preparation, and self-assessment.

Core Pillars

  • Analyze: Deep graph API integration measures communication clarity and tone in your native workflow.
  • Prepare: Leadership briefs synthesize stakeholder priorities for impact *before* key meetings.
  • Formulate: Agenda tools focus your voice using Context -> Challenge -> Action framing.
  • Improve: Transcript readers and peer feedback loops measure your performance against global leadership benchmarks.
